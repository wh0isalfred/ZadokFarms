# Zadok Farm — Stakeholder Question Bank

Use this as the source for the final Google Forms and stakeholder conversations. Its purpose is to discover how Zadok operates today, what information already exists, and what must be true before launch. It is **not** a request for Uncle Michael or staff to design screens, pick software, or answer speculative product questions.

Keep the first form short. Ask follow-up questions only when an answer reveals a real operational need.

## Form 1 — Current operations and launch reality

1. When a customer wants produce today, how do they usually contact Zadok and what happens from the first message to collection or delivery?
2. Who currently receives new customer messages, confirms availability, discusses payment, and arranges pickup or delivery? Who covers when that person is unavailable?
3. What details do you need from a customer before you can decide whether you can fulfil a produce request?
4. When stock is uncertain, how do you currently check real availability? Who makes the final decision?
5. At what point do customers receive payment details, and who confirms that payment has been received?
6. What delivery or pickup arrangements do you actually offer today? What commonly goes wrong or causes delays?
7. Are there kinds of produce, quantities, or customer requests that need a different process (for example, bulk supply, perishable items, seedlings, or livestock)?
8. How are product prices and availability currently decided and communicated when they change?
9. What records do you keep today for customers, orders, payments, stock, harvests, spoilage, training, or service enquiries? Where are they kept?
10. What parts of the current WhatsApp/phone process waste the most time, create mistakes, or get lost?
11. For training, what information must a person provide before you can decide what follow-up they need?
12. For greenhouse construction and consulting, what information makes an enquiry useful enough to respond to well?
13. What must the first launch solve immediately, and what can remain manual until later?

## Form 2 — Records and content to provide

Ask for files and examples rather than asking people to type operational data into a form.

- Current product/price spreadsheet or any existing produce list.
- Current availability/stock/harvest records, even if informal.
- Examples of real customer order conversations, with personal details removed where possible.
- Existing payment, pickup, delivery, wholesale, or perishable-hold guidance.
- Training programme information: name, purpose, audience, dates, price where applicable, delivery options, capacity, and current registration process.
- Greenhouse and consulting enquiry examples, service descriptions, and the information staff normally need before responding.
- Current farm contact details, operating locations, social links, and approved public copy.
- Real photography, logo files when finalised, and any approved documents or brand references.

## Later operational questions — keep for the system-design stage

These questions matter when Zadok is ready to make delivery, staff tools, and location handling operational—not before.

### Delivery location and fulfilment

1. Which areas can Zadok actually deliver to at launch, and which requests must be reviewed case by case?
2. Does staff currently use a map pin, typed address, phone directions, or a combination to find a customer?
3. Are delivery eligibility and fees known before a request is submitted, or only after staff review it?
4. Would storing a customer-selected map location or coordinates help staff fulfil an order? Who should be allowed to see it, and how long should it be retained?
5. What should happen when a customer cannot find their exact location on a map or has poor location accuracy?
6. Which pickup location details are approved for public display?
7. How should customers correct a delivery address after submitting?

### Orders, stock, and staff workflow

1. When should stock be reserved, released, or deducted, if at all?
2. What reservation duration is operationally realistic for perishable items?
3. Which order statuses do staff genuinely use in practice, and what event should move an order between them?
4. Which staff members need access to customer/order information, stock adjustments, training applications, and service enquiries?
5. Which actions must still work during poor connectivity, and which must wait for a live server check?

### Configuration and customer communication

1. What is the final WhatsApp business number for customer handoff?
2. Which pickup windows, delivery rules, wholesale windows, or fees should be visible to customers—and which should remain staff-confirmed?
3. Which messages need a human response, and which can be prepared automatically without making a false promise?
4. What privacy explanation is accurate for customer contact details and delivery-location data?

## Use notes

- Do not turn unresolved policies into fixed customer-facing promises.
- Do not ask stakeholders to choose map providers, database fields, or UI patterns; translate their operational answers into those decisions later.
- Date answers when collecting them. Operating rules may change with season, produce type, staffing, and delivery capacity.
